PURITAN GEMS · A CHRISTMAS GIFT

The True Christian's Love of the Unseen Christ

THOMAS VINCENT · 1677

“Whom having not seen, ye love.” — 1 Peter 1:8

Thomas Vincent was the London minister who stayed and preached through the Great Plague of 1665, when many fled. A year before he died, aged only forty-four, he wrote this warm, plain book to rekindle love for a Christ we have never seen: what that love is, why Christ is worthy of it, how to grow in it, and how Christ shows himself to those who love him.

It is free, for you to read, keep and share.
